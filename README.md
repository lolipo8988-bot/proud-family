## The entry (215 characters, 2 lines)

> My six-year-old taught Grandma to video-call tonight, then whispered,
> 'I learned it from watching you help me.'
> Grandma cried, I cried, and even the Wi-Fi behaved for once —
> kindness, passed down like a recipe card.

Why it should win: a real three-generation moment in one breath —
a child passing kindness back up the family line, a wry laugh at the
Wi-Fi finally behaving, and a specific image (kindness passed down
like a recipe card) instead of a greeting-card line. Warm, heartfelt,
and funny in the way a family would actually laugh at it.
